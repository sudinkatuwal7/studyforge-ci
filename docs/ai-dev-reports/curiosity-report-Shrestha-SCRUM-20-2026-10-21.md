## 3. Remaining race conditions

There is a window between the service’s final checks and serializer validation:

1. Service confirms selected documents are READY.
2. Service checks cited chunks against current owners/status/content.
3. Another transaction changes document status or ownership.
4. Serializer queries the chunk without checking its document’s owner/status.

A cited document becoming non-READY in that window can currently escape the serializer’s checks.

Other cases to cover:

- A selected, **uncited** document changes after the selection recheck.
- Chunk content/page changes between service verification and serializer validation.
- Document deletion or chunk replacement during generation.

Adding fresh serializer checks narrows these windows. It does **not** provide atomic protection against changes after the final database check; that requires a separately scoped transaction/locking strategy.
